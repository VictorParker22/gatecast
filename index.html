          Why am I seeing this?

          The brief is picked from four things only: the place you chose (kept on this device), the weather there (official National Weather Service alerts and the forecast at the airports near you), the US state that place is in, and a trip saved on this device, which only adds a note when an alert covers your departure. Each item says which of them put it there.

          Headlines, statehouse votes and Both sides are listed newest first, as published or recorded. They are never ranked by clicks or popularity, and clicks on them are not counted.

                  How we pick the weird

                  • Every story comes from a real outlet, and links back to it.
                  • We use the outlet’s own headline and preview image. A story opens here on Gatecast TV; the full story is always one click away at the outlet.
                  • For the editor’s picks we write a short summary in our own words. We never copy the outlet’s text.
                  • A story runs only if the strangeness is in the situation, not in someone’s misfortune.
                  • A weird rating on a card is our editor’s opinion, shown only when they gave one. The Weird-o-meter on the lead is yours.
