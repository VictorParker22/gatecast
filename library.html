    Wrote a true story? Put your book forward

    Authors and publishers can suggest a book for the true-story shelves. Before you do:

    • It has to be a true story, published, with an ISBN, and it has to fit one of the shelves.
    • Our editor reads every book put forward. If one goes on a shelf, the editor writes its description and notes; your pitch is only for us. The book then carries a small note: “Suggested by the author” or “Suggested by the publisher”.
    • There is no payment either way, and no promise of a place.
    • If you leave an email address, we write back whether or not we take the book.
    • We delete your email address 90 days after we decide, and a book we turn down 180 days after. To stop floods we keep a scrambled code of your network address, never the address itself, for 7 days.
